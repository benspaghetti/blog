[Percolation: a Mathematical Phase Transition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a-767WnbaCQ): The one that got me into percolation theory. Funnily, I didn't click on it at first when it was recommended to be by the YouTube algorithm, but it was rather insistent and kept appearing on my front page for a few weeks. Finally, I budged, and that has led me to what I'm doing now.

[Épisode 1 : Dans ma Cuisine... La percolation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AvHfL4-pj38): Percolation of water through ground coffee is probably the most common context in which this term is used in daily life.

[Percolation: Slipping through the Cracks](https://www.ams.org/publicoutreach/feature-column/fcarc-percolation): An article with more technical detail, but still easily understandable by undergrads.
