## On scaling limits in higher dimensions

A large part of the recent developments on the loop-erased random walk (LERW) have been focused on its scaling limit in three dimensions. As of now, the biggest problem is in describing this scaling limit directly in the continuum, analogous to the SLE in two dimensions. As far as I know, there is not much progress [^1].
